Story

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 is Insomniac Games' 2023 PS5-exclusive direct sequel to Marvel's Spider-Man (2018) and Spider-Man: Miles Morales (2020), structurally the bridge that has carried the franchise's two Spider-Men into shared continuity. The previous installments established Peter Parker as New York's elder Spider-Man with eight years of experience, Miles Morales as his fully-trained successor still finding his own approach to the role, and the broader Insomniac Marvel continuity's central villain network — Otto Octavius incarcerated at the Raft, Norman Osborn as the city's mayor with no public knowledge of his son Harry's Greek-coma medical situation, and Mr. Negative dismantled but not destroyed. Spider-Man 2 picks up roughly ten months later.

The opening sequence stages the franchise's first sustained dual-Spider-Man cooperation as Peter and Miles intervene against a deliberately-staged attack on the city by the mercenary Sandman. The opening battle introduces the campaign's dual-protagonist combat system — instant switching between Peter and Miles in the world map's open-zone exploration — and establishes the city's broader political infrastructure. The campaign's central reveal lands in the opening hour: Harry Osborn, Peter's lifelong best friend, has been revived from his medical coma by his father Norman through an experimental alien organism Norman has acquired via a secretive Oscorp acquisition. The organism is the Venom Symbiote.

The campaign's middle act develops three converging threads. Kraven the Hunter — a Russian aristocratic hunter dying of a terminal illness — has come to New York for a Most-Dangerous-Game-style hunt of the city's superhuman population, with his explicit goal being his own death at the hands of a worthy opponent. Kraven's hunt provides the campaign's central action structure. Peter's gradual corruption by the Venom Symbiote — initially provided to him by Harry as a therapeutic healing modality after a critical Kraven-inflicted wound — drives the campaign's psychological arc, with Peter becoming progressively aggressive, paranoid, and emotionally cruel toward MJ, Miles, and Aunt May. Miles's parallel pursuit of Martin Li — the previous game's Mr. Negative antagonist, now in custody and facing trial — anchors the campaign's continuity-grounding character work.

The campaign's structural climax lands in two converging beats. Venom decapitates Kraven in the most acclaimed mid-game sequence — the Symbiote, separated from Peter through Miles's intervention, has rebonded with Harry and consumed his sense of self. Harry, now the campaign's true Venom, escapes Oscorp's containment infrastructure and begins systematically bonding the Symbiote with the city's broader civilian population through an airborne reproductive deployment. The campaign's third-act spectacle is the city's mass-conversion sequence — Mary Jane is bonded to a derived Symbiote in one of the franchise's most acclaimed character beats, becoming the temporary antagonist Scream. The protagonists' coordinated response involves Martin Li's negotiated cooperation in severing Peter's residual Symbiote connection — a process that grants Peter the Anti-Venom powers the franchise's broader comic continuity has long maintained.

The climactic sequence stages the dual-Spider-Man confrontation with the fully-realized Venom. Harry is the central tragedy the campaign refuses to soften: the same medical intervention that saved his life has transformed him into the city's most dangerous threat. The Spider-Men disable Venom at Oscorp Tower's central reactor — Harry is left in a permanent deep coma, the Symbiote contained but not destroyed, the campaign's central emotional resolution deliberately bittersweet. The campaign's epilogue stages Peter's quiet decision to step back from the Spider-Man identity in order to spend his time with MJ and to support Harry's continuing care. Miles accepts the city's full protective responsibility. Two post-credits stingers position the franchise's third installment: Norman Osborn visits Otto Octavius at the Raft to recruit him for a planned "final act" against the Spider-Men; and Miles's mother introduces her new boyfriend's daughter Cindy Moon — the franchise's seeded protagonist Silk — for the broader continuity's continuation.
