Story

Indiana Jones and the Great Circle is MachineGames' 2024 first-person action-adventure, set in 1937 — between the events of Raiders of the Lost Ark and The Last Crusade in the film franchise's chronology — and committed to the globe-trotting tone of the original Spielberg-Lucas adventures rather than the franchise's more recent darker entries. The campaign opens at Marshall College in Connecticut, where Indy is teaching his archaeology seminar, with a sustained prologue robbery sequence at the college's antiquities collection — the franchise's signature opening-hour template — that introduces a particular Carthaginian artifact's significance to the campaign's central mystery.

Indy's investigation of the robbery sends him to the Vatican, where he encounters Italian journalist Gina Lombardi — who is independently pursuing the disappearance of her archaeologist sister Laura — and the campaign's central antagonist Emmerich Voss, a Nazi-aligned occultist whose research network has been collecting a series of seventeen specific artifacts the campaign's environmental investigation gradually identifies as the Great Circle — a pre-Antiquity power network whose components are dispersed across geographically distinct ancient sites including Vatican City, the Egyptian Pyramids of Giza, the Himalayan Sukhothai sanctuary, the Sukhothai-Pagan border highlands, and the deep desert site of Noah's Ark on Mount Sinjar.

The campaign's middle act unfolds across the franchise's signature globe-trotting structure. Indy infiltrates the Vatican's private archives, the Pyramids of Giza's underground complex, a Himalayan monastery housing one of the Great Circle's central stones, and the Sukhothai jungle's lost-civilization sites. Each location stages MachineGames' signature stealth-and-combat creative-mission design — refined across the studio's Wolfenstein trilogy — with the franchise's signature whip traversal and improvised-weapon combat woven into every encounter. The campaign's character work develops Indy and Gina's working partnership and slow-burn romantic connection across the campaign's runtime, with Gina's parallel investigation of her sister's death providing the campaign's emotional anchor.

The campaign's central reveal lands in its third act. The Great Circle is composed of seventeen stones; each stone contains a fragment of an ancient pre-civilizational power source; assembled at the original site of Noah's Ark, the stones can be used to instantaneously transport their wielder anywhere on Earth. Voss's plan is the assembly of all seventeen stones for deployment by the Nazi command structure — instantaneous global troop relocation would let the German military neutralize every major Allied position simultaneously. Indy, Gina, and their gentle giant ally Locus — a Polish strongman whose family has guarded the Great Circle's central secret across generations — pursue Voss to the desert site, accumulating the missing stones across the campaign's most acclaimed mid-game sequences.

The climactic sequence is staged aboard the recovered Noah's Ark structure, raised from the Mount Sinjar desert by Voss's coordinated excavation effort. Voss assembles the Great Circle in front of the captured protagonists and activates it. The activation does not work as planned: the Great Circle judges its wielder for worthiness, and Voss is rejected. A catastrophic atmospheric storm is triggered. Locus, in the campaign's most acclaimed character beat, recognizes that his generations-long family commitment to the Great Circle's protection has made him its only worthy possible wielder; he voluntarily takes Voss's place and uses the Circle's destabilizing power to consume both the artifact and the antagonist. Voss is burned alive by the power he attempted to seize. Locus is consumed alongside it. Indy and Gina survive the Ark's resulting collapse and wash up on the desert shore as the campaign's final image. The epilogue closes in Cairo, with Indy preparing his next expedition and Gina declining his invitation to join him — her sister's death demands she stay with the Egyptian anti-Nazi resistance — and the franchise's signature farewell-kiss closing the campaign as Indy's plane departs the runway.
