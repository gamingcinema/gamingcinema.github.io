Story

Control is Remedy Entertainment's 2019 third-person action-adventure set within the Federal Bureau of Control (FBC), a secret U.S. government agency tasked with containing supernatural and paranormal phenomena that violate the normal laws of reality. The Ultimate Edition bundles the base campaign with both story expansions — The Foundation and AWE — and the PS5 remaster ships with native 4K HDR rendering, ray tracing, and the studio's signature destructible-environment showcase. The campaign is the centerpiece of the broader Remedy Connected Universe that links Alan Wake, Quantum Break, and the upcoming Control 2; players familiar with the broader continuity will recognize the AWE expansion as the bridge between Bright Falls and the Bureau.

The campaign opens with Jesse Faden, a young woman with a quiet supernatural intuition, walking into the FBC's New York headquarters — the Oldest House, a brutalist office tower whose interior is significantly larger than its exterior and whose geometry shifts in response to its occupants. Jesse has been searching for her brother Dylan since their childhood, when an Altered World Event in their hometown of Ordinary, Wisconsin, drew Federal agents to take Dylan into custody and caused Jesse to bond psychically with an entity called Polaris — a benevolent presence from a parallel dimension that has been guiding her north since. Within minutes of her arrival, Jesse discovers the Bureau's previous Director, Zachariah Trench, has committed suicide in his office after losing control of the Hiss, a hostile parasitic resonance that has now taken over most of the Oldest House's personnel. Jesse picks up Trench's Service Weapon — a shapeshifting Object of Power that selects its bearer — and is involuntarily promoted to Director of the Bureau by the building's own occult selection ritual.

The campaign's exploration loop traverses the Oldest House's sectors — Executive, Research, Maintenance, Containment, and the dimensional Astral Plane — with each sector staging a self-contained investigation into a particular containment failure. Jesse encounters the surviving FBC personnel: Communications director Emily Pope, parautilitarian Helen Marshall, the agency's archives janitor Ahti (whose Finnish nonsense dialogue is one of the campaign's most acclaimed comic elements), and Threshold Kids researcher Dr. Casper Darling, whose recovered video logs structure the campaign's central exposition. The Bureau's bizarre operational texture — the Hotline, the Bureau-issue motel rooms, the Janitor's chores, the slide projectors that can transport agents to alternate dimensions — is rendered as the campaign's most acclaimed environmental design.

The campaign's central conflict converges on the discovery that Dylan Faden is alive, held in containment within the Oldest House, and being used as the Bureau's primary connection to the Hiss-infected dimensions. The Hiss invasion is not an external attack; it is a coordinated possession originating from Dylan himself, whose containment by the Bureau decades earlier turned him into the resonance's central host. Jesse must fight her way to him through the Ashtray Maze — the campaign's most acclaimed single sequence, staged as a sustained interior shift that rebuilds Remedy's signature environmental-distortion direction into a fully playable spectacle scored by Old Gods of Asgard's anthem "Take Control".

The campaign's closing sequence stages a deliberately ambiguous double-ending. The first credits roll over Jesse's apparent absorption into the Hiss hive mind — a fake-out designed to make the player believe the campaign has ended in failure. Then Jesse awakens in a corporate office routine, performing menial Bureau busywork while quietly recognizing that the version of reality she has been placed in is the Hiss's manufactured prison. Her connection to Polaris reasserts itself; she breaks free, returns to Dylan's containment chamber, and uses the Service Weapon to cleanse the Hiss from him at the cost of placing him in a coma. The campaign closes with Jesse formally accepting the Directorship — the Bureau no longer a place she stumbled into but a position she has committed to — and the post-credits stinger pivots directly toward the AWE expansion's Bright Falls case, the bridge between Control and the broader Alan Wake mythology Remedy has been quietly building toward since the studio's founding.
