Story

Hogwarts Legacy is Avalanche Software's 2023 open-world action-RPG, set in the Wizarding World of J.K. Rowling's Harry Potter franchise but staged a full century before Harry's birth — the late 1880s, during the Goblin Rebellion that Wizarding World canon has long referenced but never previously dramatized. The campaign's protagonist is a fully player-customized fifth-year transfer student arriving at Hogwarts under unusual circumstances: they are joining the school mid-curriculum, they have no previous magical education, and they appear to possess a unique connection to a poorly-documented form of magic the Wizarding World's central institutions have classified as Ancient Magic.

The opening sequence stages the campaign's central premise. The protagonist is being escorted to Hogwarts by Professor Eleazar Fig, a Magical Theory professor whose late wife Miriam was a leading researcher of the Ancient Magic phenomenon. Their carriage is attacked mid-flight by a dragon — a controlled assault by the goblin warlord Ranrok, who has been pursuing the Ancient Magic for reasons the campaign progressively reveals as a generations-long campaign of grievance against wizardkind. The protagonist's vision-based connection to Ancient Magic is established in the attack's aftermath, and the campaign's central investigation begins.

The campaign's middle act unfolds as a sustained Hogwarts experience layered over an open-world investigation of the Scottish Highlands. The protagonist attends classes (Charms with Professor Ronen, Potions with Professor Sharp, Defense Against the Dark Arts with the wandering auror Professor Hecat, Herbology with Professor Garlick), develops the franchise's signature magical-creature companions in the school's Room of Requirement, and conducts the central plot investigation alongside Professor Fig through the Map Chamber beneath Hogwarts. The Map Chamber's Pensieve sequences introduce four deceased Hogwarts professors — the Keepers Percival Rackham, Charles Rookwood, Niamh Fitzgerald, and San Bakar — whose recovered memories structure the campaign's central exposition.

The Keepers' memories reveal the campaign's foundational tragedy: Isidora Morganach, a young witch in the early 1700s, discovered that Ancient Magic could be used to remove emotional pain entirely from human consciousness. Her attempts to use the magic charitably — extracting her father's grief, then a series of villagers' traumas — produced increasingly dehumanized victims, and the Keepers ultimately sealed her central Repository of extracted pain to prevent its weaponization. Ranrok's pursuit of the Repository is the campaign's central political conflict: the goblin warlord has spent decades organizing a goblin rebellion specifically to access the Repository's accumulated power as retaliation for centuries of wizarding repression of goblin-kind. The campaign refuses to make either side's grievances feel illegitimate, with Ranrok's own backstory — beaten nearly to death as a child by a wizard who saw him holding a wand — providing the campaign's most acclaimed antagonist character work.

The campaign's climactic sequence stages Ranrok's successful entry to the Final Repository ahead of the protagonist. He absorbs the central pool of Ancient Magic and transforms into a dragon-form unleashed across the campaign's most demanding boss encounter. The protagonist defeats Ranrok at the cost of Professor Fig's life — Fig sustains a critical wound in the fight, with his death staged in a sustained character beat in the Repository's aftermath. The campaign closes with the protagonist's binary choice: seal the surviving Ancient Magic back away under the Keepers' original protections, or absorb the power for personal use. The choice does not affect the campaign's immediate epilogue — Fig's memorial eulogy is held at Hogwarts regardless — but it positions the protagonist's broader arc for the unannounced franchise sequel that Avalanche and Warner Bros. Games have committed to.
