About the Game

Final Fantasy VII Remake is Square Enix's 2020 ground-up reimagining of the iconic 1997 PlayStation classic's Midgar arc. Mercenary Cloud Strife joins the eco-terrorist group Avalanche in a campaign of sabotage against the Shinra Electric Power Company, whose mako reactors are draining the planet's life energy. The walkthrough captures the full main campaign — confined entirely to the dystopian megacity of Midgar — on PS5 Pro at 4K HDR 60FPS with PSSR upscaling, no commentary. The reimagined real-time action-RPG combat, expanded character work, and lavishly rebuilt cutscenes are rendered at maximum visual fidelity.

Walkthrough — Chapter Breakdown

A complete chapter-by-chapter overview of the campaign. Use this guide to track story progress, locate specific moments in the videos above, or plan a multi-session viewing.

  1. 1

    1. The Destruction of Mako Reactor 1

    The campaign opens with Cloud Strife, an ex-SOLDIER mercenary, hired by the eco-terrorist resistance group Avalanche for a sabotage mission against Shinra's Mako Reactor 1. The chapter introduces the campaign's real-time action-RPG combat fundamentals — the ATB gauge, the tactical pause menu, and the punishing Scorpion Sentinel boss encounter that closes the chapter. The opening sequence weaves combat tutorial with the campaign's central thematic question: whether Shinra's planetary exploitation can be stopped without harming Midgar's working-class residents.

  2. 2

    2. Fateful Encounters

    Cloud, separated from Avalanche during the post-bombing chaos, meets the flower seller Aerith Gainsborough in the Sector 8 plaza. Pursued by Shinra's Public Security division, the two flee through Midgar's upper-plate marketplace in the campaign's first sustained character chapter. The chapter ends with Cloud falling through a church roof in the Sector 5 undercity slums, separated from Aerith, the campaign's central character network beginning to form.

  3. 3

    3. Home Sweet Slum

    Cloud, returned to Avalanche's Sector 7 hideout, reunites with childhood friend Tifa Lockhart and the resistance leader Barret Wallace. The chapter dwells on the campaign's domestic texture — the slum residents' working-class lives, the Seventh Heaven bar Avalanche operates as a cover, and the relationships that ground the campaign's central political stakes. The chapter ends with Cloud accepting Avalanche's next contract: the destruction of Mako Reactor 5.

  4. 4

    4. Mad Dash

    A short transitional chapter staged as a motorcycle escort through the Sector 4 plate underbelly. Cloud, Tifa, and Barret traverse the city's massive structural support framework on the way to Reactor 5, with the franchise's signature mid-mission spectacle and combat against Shinra's mid-air defense forces. The chapter ends with the team's arrival at Reactor 5's exterior approach.

  5. 5

    5. Dogged Pursuit

    The infiltration of Mako Reactor 5. The chapter is structured as a sustained interior exploration sequence with the campaign's most demanding mid-game combat encounters. The chapter ends with the bombing successful but the team caught in a Shinra ambush — the campaign's central political reveal staged as the reactor's true defense network is unveiled.

  6. 6

    6. Light the Way

    A short exploration chapter set in the slums following the Reactor 5 mission. Cloud, separated from his Avalanche allies, navigates the Sector 5 streets and reunites with Aerith at her childhood home. The chapter dwells on Aerith's character — her relationship with her adoptive mother Elmyra, her quiet awareness of her Cetra heritage, and her growing concern for Cloud's deteriorating mental state.

  7. 7

    7. A Trip Down Memory Lane

    Cloud and Aerith, traveling back toward Sector 7, are forced to take refuge in Sector 6's Wall Market — Midgar's most notorious black-market district controlled by the crime lord Don Corneo. The chapter is one of the campaign's most acclaimed expansions of the original: the Don Corneo audition sequence, with Cloud crossdressing to infiltrate Corneo's mansion, is staged with significantly more character depth than the 1997 original. The chapter ends with Tifa, Cloud, and Aerith escaping the mansion together, with critical intelligence about Shinra's plans for Sector 7.

  8. 8

    8. Just Flowers for a Friend

    A short character-focused chapter as the team prepares for Shinra's imminent attack on Sector 7. The chapter weaves quiet domestic scenes with the campaign's mounting political tension. The chapter ends with Shinra's plate-drop operation initiated — Sector 7's support pillar will be destroyed, with the entire upper plate collapsing onto the undercity slums below.

  9. 9

    9. The Town That Never Sleeps

    The campaign's most demanding mid-game combat chapter. The team fights through Shinra's military forces in a desperate ascent of the Sector 7 support pillar, attempting to prevent the plate drop. The chapter is staged as a sustained vertical combat sequence with the franchise's most acclaimed mid-game boss encounter. The chapter ends with Avalanche's leadership (Biggs, Wedge, Jessie) killed in the operation, the plate drop unprevented, and Sector 7 destroyed.

  10. 10

    10. Rough Waters

    The campaign's emotional pivot chapter. Cloud, Tifa, and Barret, having failed to prevent the plate drop, escape Sector 7 through the city's drainage infrastructure. The chapter dwells on the team's grief and the campaign's reframed political stakes. The chapter ends with Aerith captured by Shinra's elite Turks unit, the team's next mission committed: infiltrate Shinra's central headquarters in the upper plate.

  11. 11

    11. Haywire

    The infiltration of Shinra Headquarters' lower floors. The chapter is structured as a sustained corporate-tower stealth and combat sequence, with the franchise's signature elevator-based combat encounters. The chapter dwells on Shinra's internal corporate texture — the office workers, the scientists, the executives — as the campaign's central antagonist is humanized for the first time.

  12. 12

    12. Fight for Survival

    The Shinra Headquarters upper floors. The chapter weaves combat with the campaign's most acclaimed exposition sequences — President Shinra's penthouse, the executive boardroom, and the introduction of the campaign's central antagonist Rufus Shinra. The chapter ends with the team's escape from Shinra Headquarters on the Sector 0 expressway, the campaign's central political conflict fully exposed.

  13. 13

    13. The Day Midgar Stood Still

    The campaign's climax. The team confronts Sephiroth — the campaign's central supernatural antagonist — in a sustained battle staged across multiple cinematic environments. The chapter weaves combat with the campaign's most acclaimed cutscene direction, with Cloud's deteriorating mental state and Sephiroth's manipulation of the campaign's central reality fully foregrounded. The chapter ends with the team escaping Midgar on motorcycles, the broader Final Fantasy VII narrative committed for the remake trilogy's second installment.
