Story

Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced is Ubisoft's full remake of Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag, released on July 9, 2026 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC, thirteen years after the original. Led by Ubisoft Singapore, it is rebuilt from the ground up on the latest version of the Anvil engine, with more detailed character faces, richer animation and denser crowds. The team describes it as a faithful remake that keeps the original's single-player adventure at its center rather than turning it into an RPG.

The story is unchanged in its essentials. You play Edward Kenway, a Welsh privateer who turns to piracy in the early eighteenth-century Caribbean, and who is gradually drawn into the long-running conflict between the Assassins and the Templar Order. Kenway is the grandfather of Connor from Assassin's Creed III, and his path crosses historical figures of the Golden Age of Piracy, among them Blackbeard and Mary Read, across the pirate haven of Nassau, Havana, Kingston and the islands in between.

Gameplay has been reworked rather than simply repainted. Combat is rebuilt around parries and takedowns, with a rope dart and pistol moves and a new enemy type, the Demolitionist. Stealth is more forgiving, with crouch-anywhere and dive-anywhere movement and an Eagle Vision that gains an Observe mode, and parkour gains manual jumps and side and back ejects for smoother escapes and assassinations.

The sea is still the heart of the game. Edward's ship, the Jackdaw, can be customized more deeply, with new secondary weapons such as shrapnel barrels and 8-pounders and recruitable officers who bring unique abilities, and naval combat, boarding actions and fort assaults tie the open world together. Visually the remake adds ray-traced lighting, dynamic weather, modernized water and destructible environments, and French musician Woodkid contributes new music and sea shanties.

On PlayStation 5 the game supports DualSense haptic feedback and adaptive triggers, Tempest 3D Audio and multiple graphics modes, with ray tracing pushed further across all modes on both PS5 and PS5 Pro and additional PS5 Pro enhancements. The original Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag remains available alongside the remake.

The walkthrough captures the full campaign in continuous cinematic form, presented without commentary on PS5 Pro. For viewers new to the series: Black Flag is widely regarded as one of the series' most beloved entries, because its pirate adventure stands largely on its own, and Resynced presents it with modern fidelity.
