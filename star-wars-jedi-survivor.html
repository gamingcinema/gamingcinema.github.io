Story

Star Wars Jedi: Survivor is Respawn Entertainment's 2023 PS5/PC sequel to Jedi: Fallen Order, set five years after the previous campaign's events — the canonical date 9 BBY, five years before the events of A New Hope. The previous campaign closed with Cal Kestis destroying the Force-sensitive children Holocron in the Mantis's escape from Fortress Inquisitorius, his survival against Darth Vader staged with deliberate ambiguity, and the broader Jedi-resistance infrastructure positioned for his continuing mission. Survivor opens with Cal in his late twenties, scarred by the intervening years, and operating as one of the Hidden Path's most experienced resistance operatives.

The opening sequence stages a coordinated Imperial-archives heist on Coruscant. Cal infiltrates the Imperial Security Bureau's central tower in pursuit of classified Imperial intelligence on the Pau'an species's coordinated genocide — information that the broader Hidden Path resistance needs to organize a humanitarian-evacuation operation. The mission goes wrong, with Cal's resistance team killed by the High Republic-era Imperial Senator Sejan, and Cal himself narrowly escapes Coruscant aboard the crippled Mantis. He arrives on the frontier planet Koboh — a sustained open-zone environment that anchors the campaign's central exploration loop — and meets the mercenary Bode Akuna, a sharpshooter whose stated commitments to his eight-year-old daughter Kata make him the campaign's central supporting character.

The campaign's central artifact is the Compass of Tanalorr, a pre-Republic navigation device that contains the only known path through the Koboh Abyss — a hyperspace storm that has historically protected the lost planet Tanalorr from Imperial detection. Tanalorr is the campaign's central political prize: a planet hidden from Imperial reach that the Hidden Path could establish as a centralized resistance refuge. The campaign's antagonist faction is initially the High Republic-era Jedi Dagan Gera, who has spent two centuries in stasis after the original Tanalorr base's collapse and who emerges in the campaign's first act with his own conflicting plan to claim the planet as the central node of a resurrected dark-side Jedi order.

The campaign's middle act develops Cal, Bode, Greez, and Cere's coordinated investigation across Koboh's surface, the Pyloon's Saloon central settlement, the resurrected mountain fortress Jedha, and the deeper Koboh underground infrastructure. Cere is killed by Bode in the campaign's most acclaimed midgame structural twist. Bode has been an Imperial Security Bureau mole for the entire campaign — his daughter Kata is the Empire's leverage against him, his wife was killed by the Empire as initial punishment for his early Jedi sympathies, and his stated commitment to a peaceful farming retirement on Tanalorr is the cover for his actual plan to deliver the Compass to the Empire in exchange for Kata's protected exile. Bode is himself a hidden Jedi — his Force abilities have been entirely suppressed across the campaign's runtime — and his betrayal of Cal at the moment the Compass is repaired is the campaign's central reveal.

The campaign's third act unfolds across an Imperial Security Bureau base on the planet Nova Garon, where Bode has taken refuge with Kata and the recovered Compass. Cal infiltrates the base, recovers the Compass, and confronts Bode in a sustained final boss encounter staged across the base's collapsing infrastructure. Bode's lightsaber — an orange-bladed weapon he has kept hidden for the entire campaign — is the campaign's most acclaimed visual reveal. Cal defeats Bode in single combat. Bode dies, asking Cal to protect Kata from the Empire's continuing pursuit. The campaign closes with Cal, Greez, and Kata's coordinated departure for Tanalorr aboard the rebuilt Mantis, with the broader Hidden Path resistance infrastructure positioned for the franchise's third installment — and the broader Star Wars Original Trilogy timeline's events approaching across the campaign's epilogue.
