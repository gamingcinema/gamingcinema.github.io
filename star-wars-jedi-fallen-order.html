Story

Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order is Respawn Entertainment's 2019 third-person action-adventure, set in the Star Wars canon's five years after Order 66 — the systematic Jedi-extermination protocol that terminated the Jedi Order at the close of the prequel trilogy. The campaign protagonist is Cal Kestis, a former Jedi Padawan who survived Order 66 as a child by using his master's coordinated sacrifice as cover for his own escape. Cal has spent the intervening years working as a scrap-yard rigger on the Outer Rim planet Bracca, deliberately suppressing his Force abilities and avoiding any behavior that might attract Imperial attention.

The opening sequence stages the campaign's inciting incident. Cal's coworker and closest friend Prauf is about to fall to his death during a routine industrial accident; Cal uses Force telekinesis to save him in front of multiple witnesses. The Empire's Inquisitorius — the order of fallen Jedi tasked with hunting surviving Jedi — dispatches the Second Sister and the Ninth Sister to Bracca within hours. Prauf is murdered by the Inquisitors as a public demonstration. Cal is rescued from the resulting chase by the crew of the Stinger Mantis: former Jedi Knight Cere Junda (whose own complicated Order 66 backstory the campaign progressively reveals), Latero pilot Greez Dritus, and the salvaged astromech droid BD-1 whose recovered memory provides the campaign's central exposition.

The campaign's central artifact is a Jedi Holocron containing a comprehensive list of Force-sensitive children across the galaxy — children whose identification by either the Jedi or the Empire would determine whether the Order could be rebuilt or systematically wiped out. The Holocron has been hidden by Cere's former Jedi Master Eno Cordova in a coordinated multi-planet trail of clues only Cere's apprentice could follow. Cal's mission is the Holocron's recovery ahead of the Inquisitorius's parallel pursuit. The campaign's exploration unfolds across Bogano, Zeffo, Kashyyyk, Dathomir, and Ilum, with the franchise's signature lightsaber-combat system and the campaign's Metroidvania-style environmental progression rendering the planets as interconnected sustained exploration zones.

The campaign's central character reveal lands at the midpoint. The Second Sister — the Inquisitor pursuing the Mantis crew across the campaign — is Trilla Suduri, Cere's own former Jedi Padawan. During Order 66, Cere had been captured with Trilla and a group of younglings; under Inquisitorius torture, Cere broke and revealed Trilla's location to the Empire, with Trilla subsequently captured, tortured to the breaking point, and turned to the Dark Side as a sanctioned Inquisitor. Cere's lifelong avoidance of Force use is the suppressed grief over what she did to her own apprentice. The campaign's broader character work tracks Cere's slow recovery of her Jedi identity through Cal's mentorship.

The climactic sequence stages a coordinated assault on Fortress Inquisitorius on the Outer Rim planet Nur. Cal defeats Trilla in single combat in the Fortress's central reflection chamber. Cere — arriving moments later — speaks with Trilla in the language of their old apprenticeship and convinces her, in the campaign's most acclaimed character beat, to renounce her Inquisitorius commitments. Trilla's resulting redemption is interrupted by the arrival of Darth Vader himself, whose presence the campaign refuses to dramatize as a boss fight. Vader strikes Trilla down with one move, throws Cere across the chamber, and pursues Cal through the collapsing Fortress in a sustained chase sequence. Cal escapes by flooding the Fortress's central waterway, barely surviving Vader's pursuit, and the campaign closes on the Mantis's escape with the Holocron intact. Cal's final decision is the campaign's central moral resolution: he destroys the Holocron, refusing to position the named Force-sensitive children for either rebuilding or extermination, leaving their futures to the Force. The franchise's broader Sequel Trilogy continuity is positioned for the 2023 successor installment.
