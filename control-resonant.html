Story

Control Resonant is Remedy Entertainment's direct sequel to the 2019 supernatural hit Control, and by the studio's own description its most expansive game to date. Released on September 24, 2026 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC, it is self-published by Remedy and sits within the studio's connected universe alongside Alan Wake.

The campaign swaps protagonists. Instead of Jesse Faden, the player controls Dylan Faden, her younger brother, whom the original game presented as an antagonist. As a child Dylan was taken by the Federal Bureau of Control and raised inside an institution that tried to dictate his life, a story that ended in tragedy and left him contained for years. Jesse stopped him and cleansed him of the force he carried, but he fell into a coma; he now wakes in an unfamiliar world with Jesse missing. The Bureau deploys him at the peak of a crisis: the Hiss has broken containment and overrun Manhattan, and Dylan must find his sister and bring the fight to it.

Dylan is joined by a new Bureau ally, Zoe de Vera, alongside returning favorites Simon Arish and Emily Pope, with Dr. Casper Darling also returning, and is played by Sean Durrie. Combat leans more heavily on melee than the first game: Dylan wields a shape-shifting weapon that can become a hammer, a scythe or dual blades in an instant. The setting moves the series' architecture-as-antagonist approach out of the Oldest House and into a warped Manhattan of impossible geometry, shifting realities and monstrous entities born from a new kind of resonance.

The first Control (2019) followed Jesse Faden as she took charge of the Federal Bureau of Control and fought the Hiss inside the Oldest House, a shifting brutalist headquarters in New York, using the supernatural Service Weapon and telekinetic powers. It became a cornerstone of Remedy's connected universe, which links Control with the Alan Wake games — and the expansion content of Alan Wake 2 carried a direct teaser for this sequel. Resonant carries that thread forward while moving the action out of a single building and into an entire city.
