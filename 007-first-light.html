About the Game

007 First Light is IO Interactive's 2026 origin-story James Bond title, the studio's first AAA project outside the Hitman franchise. The campaign stages a younger James Bond — newly promoted to 00 status — across a globe-trotting espionage thriller that explores the character's formative early MI6 years. The walkthrough captures the full main campaign on PS5 Pro at 4K HDR 60FPS with PSSR upscaling, no commentary. IO Interactive's signature creative-mission design — refined across three Hitman trilogies — is applied to the spy-thriller genre, with the campaign's combat, stealth, and environmental investigation mechanics rendered with the studio's hallmark sandbox approach.
