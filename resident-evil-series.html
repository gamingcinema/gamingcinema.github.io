Story

Resident Evil 2 Remake is Capcom's 2019 ground-up reimagining of the 1998 PlayStation classic, built on the studio's RE Engine and structured as a survival-horror foundation for the modern Resident Evil franchise. The campaign opens on the night of September 29, 1998, in Raccoon City — the Midwestern American city the franchise's broader continuity has established as the site of a catastrophic Umbrella Corporation T-virus outbreak. The Raccoon City Police Department has been overrun. The civilian population has been almost entirely infected. The campaign opens with two protagonists who do not know each other arriving in the city in parallel: rookie police officer Leon S. Kennedy, beginning his first day on the force; and college student Claire Redfield, traveling to Raccoon City to find her missing brother Chris.

Leon and Claire's first encounter is at a gas station on the city's outskirts, where their attempt to escape the spreading infection is interrupted by the first sustained zombie engagement of the campaign. They reach Raccoon City separately after the truck they are sharing is destroyed in a collision, and the campaign splits between two parallel playable scenarios — Leon's A scenario infiltrating the RPD building from the front and progressing through the police laboratory infrastructure, and Claire's B scenario entering the RPD building through the parking garage and progressing through the orphanage facility infrastructure. The campaign deliberately encourages multiple playthroughs: each protagonist's scenario reveals environmental context the other scenario elides.

The campaign's middle act develops through the RPD building's exploration — the franchise's most acclaimed survival-horror environment, with the campaign's signature Mr. X / T-103 Tyrant pursuing the player across the RPD's three floors as a continuous environmental threat. Leon's scenario partners him with Ada Wong, a mysterious investigator whose stated FBI affiliation is contradicted by her broader behavior; Claire's scenario partners her with Sherry Birkin, the twelve-year-old daughter of the Umbrella scientist responsible for the broader outbreak. The campaign's central reveal lands in the RPD basement: Dr. William Birkin, Sherry's father, has injected himself with the experimental G-virus and is now pursuing the protagonists in increasingly mutated mid-stage forms across the campaign's underground sections.

The campaign's third act traverses the NEST — Umbrella's central Raccoon City research laboratory, accessible through the city sewer system and the central police building's hidden elevator infrastructure. The protagonists' parallel paths converge inside NEST, with both scenarios closing on a coordinated escape via the facility's underground emergency train. The campaign's central antagonist sequence is the protagonists' final confrontation with the fully-mutated Birkin G-form, staged aboard the emergency train as Birkin's final mutation breaks through the rear carriage's structural integrity. Leon, in the climactic sequence, uses a maintenance pipe to stab the Birkin G-form's exposed eye in coordination with Claire's detachment of the rear carriage. Birkin is consumed by the NEST self-destruct sequence's spreading flames; the train reaches the surface; Leon, Claire, and Sherry escape on foot into the surrounding wilderness.

The campaign closes with the three survivors walking together down a dirt road as a vehicle approaches from the distance. The driver — uninfected, oblivious to Raccoon City's collapse — passes without stopping. The campaign refuses to provide a satisfying institutional resolution: there is no government rescue, no Umbrella reckoning, no formal closure of the broader outbreak the campaign has documented. Leon vows to expose Umbrella's broader complicity in the Raccoon City incident, Claire vows to continue her search for Chris, and Sherry — now Claire's adoptive responsibility — is the campaign's emotional anchor for the franchise's broader continuity. The franchise's Raccoon City arc is closed; the broader Umbrella reckoning is positioned for the franchise's subsequent installments.

Story

Resident Evil 3 Remake is Capcom's 2020 PS5/PS4 follow-up to Resident Evil 2 Remake, set across September 28-30, 1998 — the same Raccoon City outbreak documented in the previous installment, but staged from a parallel protagonist's perspective that overlaps with Leon and Claire's RPD investigation. The protagonist is Jill Valentine, the surviving member of the Raccoon City Police Department's S.T.A.R.S. special tactics unit and the previous Resident Evil (1996) campaign's central protagonist. Jill has spent the months between Resident Evil and Resident Evil 3 in self-imposed apartment confinement, attempting to publicly expose Umbrella's responsibility for the original Spencer Mansion incident and being systematically discredited by Umbrella's PR infrastructure.

The campaign opens on the evening of September 28, with the broader Raccoon City T-virus outbreak two days into its exponential phase. Jill is asleep in her apartment when her S.T.A.R.S. colleague Brad Vickers calls in panic — Umbrella has dispatched a specialized bio-organic weapon to eliminate every surviving S.T.A.R.S. operative, and the unit is hunting Jill specifically. The opening sequence is structured as a sustained chase: Nemesis, the bio-organic weapon, breaks through Jill's apartment wall in the franchise's most iconic single set-piece. Jill escapes the apartment by the fire escape, with the campaign's signature evasive-traversal mechanics introduced in the opening fifteen minutes.

The campaign's middle act traverses Raccoon City's downtown districts as Jill attempts to reach the city's central evacuation infrastructure. She is rescued from a Nemesis encounter on the Redstone Street subway platform by Carlos Oliveira, a member of Umbrella's private rescue corps (the U.B.C.S., a private mercenary subdivision deployed as the company's public-facing humanitarian response while Umbrella's broader research division covers up the company's actual culpability for the outbreak). Carlos's working relationship with Jill is the campaign's central character thread, with his slow recognition of his employer's broader complicity providing the campaign's political grounding. Carlos's parallel playable sections — including a sustained RPD building infiltration that interlocks with RE2 Remake's scenarios — give the campaign its multi-protagonist texture.

The campaign's third act centers on the synthesis of a t-virus vaccine at NEST-2, Umbrella's secondary Raccoon City research facility hidden beneath the city hospital. Jill, recovering from a brief Nemesis-induced coma, learns from a television broadcast that the United States government has authorized a classified surgical strike on Raccoon City — a thermobaric weapons deployment that will incinerate the entire metropolitan area to contain the outbreak. The vaccine synthesis becomes the campaign's central race against the surgical strike's countdown. Jill navigates NEST-2's research infrastructure with Nemesis in continuous environmental pursuit, with the bio-weapon's accumulating injuries gradually transforming it into increasingly distorted mutated forms.

The climactic sequence stages Jill's final confrontation with the fully-mutated Nemesis in NEST-2's disposal center. The bio-weapon is killed by Jill's improvised use of an experimental railgun weapon system, with the franchise's signature final-boss combat closing in a sustained one-on-one encounter. The vaccine is briefly stolen by Umbrella's Russian operative Nikolai Zinoviev before Carlos recovers it. The thermobaric strike incinerates Raccoon City in the campaign's epilogue, with Jill, Carlos, and the surviving rescue personnel escaping the city perimeter aboard a helicopter as the entire downtown area is destroyed in a sustained sequence the campaign refuses to soften. The campaign closes with Jill's stated commitment to spend the rest of her life dismantling Umbrella from the outside, with the broader franchise's anti-Umbrella resistance network — the BSAA that Resident Evil 5 would eventually establish — positioned for the broader continuity's subsequent installments.

Story

Resident Evil 4 Remake is Capcom's 2023 PS5/PS4 ground-up reimagining of the 2005 GameCube original — the franchise's most acclaimed installment, structurally separated from the Raccoon City arc by six years and committed to a tonal reinvention of the franchise's survival-horror conventions toward action-horror pacing. The campaign opens in 2004, six years after the Raccoon City incident. Leon S. Kennedy, the rookie police officer from Resident Evil 2, has spent the intervening years training under the United States Secret Service and is now the President's elite personal-detail agent. The President's twenty-year-old daughter Ashley Graham has been kidnapped by an unidentified European cult. Leon is the President's personal selection for the rescue operation.

The campaign opens on a remote village in rural Spain, where Leon's two Spanish escorts are murdered by the village's residents — civilians whose behavior has been corrupted by an ancient parasitic organism called Las Plagas. The village's chief Bitores Méndez — a towering, religiously-fervent figure — captures Leon and forcibly injects him with a Las Plagas embryo. The campaign's central biological premise is established: Leon now carries the parasitic infection, with the embryo's mature manifestation timed for the campaign's late-game sequences. He has approximately seventy-two hours, in-fiction, before complete possession by the parasite. The campaign's clock is now running.

The campaign's middle act traverses the village's surrounding territories — the rural Spanish landscape, the abandoned church complex where Ashley is being held, and the central castle of the antagonist Ramón Salazar, an aged inheritor of the Las Plagas-aligned Los Iluminados cult. Leon rescues Ashley from the church but is immediately recaptured by Salazar's forces during their escape. The campaign's signature mid-game expansion is Ashley's deepening characterization — the 2005 original treated Ashley primarily as an escort-mission liability, while the Remake foregrounds her ingenuity, her working partnership with Leon, and her own infection with a Las Plagas embryo. The campaign's chemist supporting character Luis Serra Navarro — a former Umbrella researcher who joined Los Iluminados attempting to study Las Plagas from inside, then defected after recognizing the cult's broader weaponization plan — provides the campaign's central exposition and the protagonists' Plagas suppressant supply.

The campaign's third act unfolds across Salazar's central castle complex and the broader island fortress controlled by the cult's true leader, Lord Osmund Saddler. The campaign's central political reveal arrives in the castle's audience hall: Saddler's plan is the systematic infection of the United States executive branch via Ashley as a returning carrier, with the eventual goal of using the President's authority to establish Los Iluminados as the country's new governing religious infrastructure. Salazar is killed in the castle complex's collapse sequence; Luis is killed by Saddler shortly afterward, with his death providing the campaign's emotional anchor. The island fortress's research facilities reveal Umbrella's residual involvement with Las Plagas research — the company's collapse after Raccoon City did not actually terminate its weaponization programs.

The climactic sequence stages Leon's confrontation with Saddler's fully-mutated final form. The fight is staged across the island fortress's central courtyard, with the franchise's signature action-horror combat closing in a sustained multi-phase encounter. Saddler is killed; the Las Plagas hierarchical control mechanism is broken; Leon and Ashley's parasitic embryos are extracted using Luis's stockpiled extraction equipment. The campaign closes on the franchise's most acclaimed closing sequence: Leon and Ashley escape the collapsing island on a recovered jet ski moments before the thermobaric self-destruct sequence detonates, with the franchise's signature playful banter — Leon's gentle deflection of Ashley's polite suggestion of a drink — closing the campaign as the jet ski accelerates into the Mediterranean sunrise. The franchise's broader Las Plagas continuity — Resident Evil 5 and Resident Evil 6's broader bioterrorism arc — is positioned for the franchise's subsequent installments.

Story

Resident Evil Requiem is Capcom's 2026 ninth main installment of the long-running survival-horror franchise — the direct successor to Resident Evil Village (2021), set twenty-eight years after the original Raccoon City Destruction Incident and structurally committed to closing the franchise's broader Spencer-Umbrella continuity. The campaign's protagonist is Grace Ashcroft, an FBI intelligence analyst whose personal connection to the franchise's foundational events is the central premise: Grace is the daughter of investigative journalist Alyssa Ashcroft, a Raccoon City outbreak survivor whose subsequent investigation into Umbrella's continuing operations led to her unsolved murder eight years before the campaign's present.

The campaign opens in October 2026 at the abandoned Wrenwood Hotel — the site of Alyssa's murder, now repurposed as the crime scene for a fifth victim in a string of mysterious deaths across the United States. Four of the previous victims were former Raccoon City outbreak survivors. Grace's supervisor, Special Agent Nathan Dempsy, has approved her involvement in the investigation specifically because of her personal connection — Dempsy sympathizes with her unresolved grief and recognizes the broader political pattern the murders point toward. Grace's investigation of the Wrenwood Hotel scene transitions from procedural FBI work into the franchise's signature survival-horror exploration when she is captured by Dr. Victor Gideon, a former Umbrella researcher whose post-collapse private operation has been reconstituting the company's central research infrastructure.

The campaign's structural decision is its dual-protagonist split between Grace and Leon S. Kennedy, the franchise's long-running secondary protagonist. Grace's sections continue the franchise's first-person survival-horror gameplay established in Resident Evil 7: Biohazard (2017), staged at Gideon's Rhodes Hill Chronic Care Center — a long-term care facility that has been operating as a continuous Umbrella experimentation site. Leon's sections are action-oriented in the Resident Evil 4 tradition, investigating the murders' broader political pattern from the outside. The two protagonists converge inside the Rhodes Hill facility, with Leon rescuing Grace from her captivity and joining her central investigation. Their working partnership grounds the campaign's middle act.

The campaign's central reveal lands at the midpoint. Grace is the adopted daughter of Oswell E. Spencer, the co-founder of Umbrella Corporation and the franchise's broader continuity-anchoring antagonist figure. Spencer, in his post-collapse final years, took custody of orphaned Grace as a deliberate act of atonement for the Raccoon City Orphanage cloning experiments his company had conducted. Dr. Gideon's central operational error is his belief that Grace was one of the original Orphanage clones — making her, in his theory, the genetic key to Elpis, a mass-deployable consciousness-modification bioweapon Umbrella had been developing in the years before its collapse. Grace's actual genetics are irrelevant to Elpis; Gideon's pursuit of her has been a fundamental misidentification.

The campaign's third act unfolds at the ARK Laboratory, Umbrella's hidden secondary research facility off the American coast. Grace deploys Elpis at the facility's central reactor — and discovers, in the campaign's most acclaimed structural reveal, that Elpis is not the consciousness-modification bioweapon Umbrella had advertised internally. It is an antiviral agent capable of reversing Umbrella's prior experimentation. The facility's surviving experimental subjects — including the campaign's secondary playable character Emily, a young Umbrella-engineered survivor Grace has been protecting throughout the campaign — are cured by Elpis's deployment. Gideon and the campaign's manipulating shadow figure Zeno are killed in the ARK Laboratory's resulting collapse. Grace and Leon are extracted by Chris Redfield's BSAA team. The campaign closes with Grace's formal adoption of Emily as her daughter, the franchise's broader Umbrella continuity finally closed by the antiviral's success, and the post-credits stinger positioning the broader Resident Evil franchise's continuation for the studio's next-generation Capcom production cycle.
