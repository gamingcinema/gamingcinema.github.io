About the Game

Uncharted 3: Drake's Deception is Naughty Dog's 2011 PS3 third installment, the campaign that fully foregrounded Nathan Drake's friendship with Victor Sullivan and the franchise's central character question — who is Drake outside of his adventuring. The story follows Drake and Sully across Cartagena, London, Yemen, Syria, and the lost city of Iram of the Pillars beneath the Rub' al Khali desert, hunted by Katherine Marlowe's secret society. The walkthrough captures the full campaign on PS5 Pro via backwards compatibility, no commentary.

Walkthrough — Chapter Breakdown

A complete chapter-by-chapter overview of the campaign. Use this guide to track story progress, locate specific moments in the videos above, or plan a multi-session viewing.

  1. 1

    1. Another Round — Cartagena Pub

    The campaign opens in a Cartagena pub where Drake and Sully are negotiating the sale of Sir Francis Drake's ring — Nathan's most personal artifact — to a buyer working for the antagonist Katherine Marlowe. The opening sequence is staged as a sustained pub brawl, with the franchise's signature improvised combat introduced through the cramped interior. The chapter ends with Marlowe's organization in active pursuit and the campaign's central artifact — Sir Francis Drake's lost expedition logbook — confirmed as the antagonists' goal.

  2. 2

    2. London — Marlowe's Library

    Drake and Sully travel to London to investigate Marlowe's secret-society headquarters, hidden beneath an unassuming city library. The chapter is structured as a sustained stealth infiltration, with the franchise's signature climbing sequences staged across London's historic rooftops. The chapter ends with the next clue recovered, pointing to the lost Atlantis of the Sands — the legendary city of Iram of the Pillars.

  3. 3

    3. The Yemen Souk

    An open-zone chapter set in a Yemeni city marketplace, with Drake pursuing the next Iram clue through a dense urban environment. The chapter weaves the franchise's signature traversal across rooftops and souk stalls with sustained combat against Marlowe's pursuing forces. The chapter dwells on the campaign's character work — Sully's deepening protective instincts toward Drake and the franchise's first sustained look at their working friendship.

  4. 4

    4. The Cruise Ship

    The campaign's most acclaimed mid-game set-piece. Drake, captured by Marlowe's forces, is held aboard a cruise ship in a Mediterranean harbor. The chapter is structured as a sustained ship infiltration, with the ship's hull taking progressive flood damage during the campaign's climactic battle. The chapter's central sequence — Drake fighting through a sinking ship's interior as the entire environment rotates ninety degrees and floods — has become canonical to the franchise's reputation.

  5. 5

    5. Syria — The Citadel

    Drake and Sully travel to Syria to investigate a Crusader-era citadel containing the next Iram clue. The chapter is structured as a sustained exploration sequence with the franchise's signature climbing and puzzle-solving fundamentals. The chapter ends with the next clue confirmed and the path to the Rub' al Khali desert committed.

  6. 6

    6. The Plane Crash

    The campaign's most iconic single sequence. Drake, infiltrating a cargo plane mid-flight, fights through the plane's interior as the aircraft suffers catastrophic damage and breaks apart in mid-air. The sequence ends with Drake parachuting alone into the Rub' al Khali desert, isolated from Sully and the rest of the supporting cast. The chapter has become canonical to the franchise's reputation for staging impossible action in real-time playable form.

  7. 7

    7. Desert — Walking Sequence

    The campaign's most acclaimed character chapter. Drake, alone in the Rub' al Khali desert, walks toward the rumored location of Iram with no combat, no music, and progressive heat-stroke hallucinations. The chapter is one of the franchise's most studied design decisions — replacing the conventional combat-heavy mid-game pacing with a sustained character-survival sequence. The chapter ends with Drake collapsing in the dunes as a Bedouin caravan finds him.

  8. 8

    8. The Bedouin Caravan

    Drake, rescued by a local Bedouin tribe led by Salim, recovers from his desert ordeal and rejoins the central investigation. Salim's tribe has its own historical investment in Iram of the Pillars — the lost city is the site of an ancient massacre that Salim's people remember in oral history. The chapter ends with Drake, Salim, and a Bedouin war party riding for Iram in pursuit of Sully and Marlowe.

  9. 9

    9. Iram of the Pillars — The Lost City

    The campaign's third-act spectacle chapter. The lost city of Iram, hidden beneath the Rub' al Khali sands, is rendered as one of the franchise's most architecturally ambitious environments. The chapter weaves combat against Marlowe's forces with the franchise's signature treasure-hunting exploration, with the city's central reveal — that Iram's curse is a hallucinogenic vapor from the city's well — driving the campaign's final act.

  10. 10

    10. The Brass Vessel — Climax

    The campaign's climax. Drake confronts Marlowe and her enforcer Talbot in Iram's central chamber, fighting through hallucinogenic visions as the city's seismic instability collapses around the party. The chapter ends with Marlowe killed by the city's quicksand, Talbot defeated, and Drake and Sully's escape staged with the city sinking into the desert behind them. The credits roll on the franchise's most personal character resolution between Drake and Sully.
