Story

Darksiders Warmastered Edition is the remastered version of Darksiders, the 2010 action-adventure created at Vigil Games, released on November 22, 2016 for PlayStation 4 and Xbox One by publisher THQ Nordic, with a PC release a week later. The remaster was handled by the studio KAIKO and brings native 1080p rendering, doubled texture resolution, improved shadows and post-processing, and 60 frames per second in moment-to-moment gameplay, with optional 4K support on PS4 Pro and PC.

The story begins with the apocalypse arriving too soon. You are War, the first of the Four Horsemen, who is deceived into bringing about the end of the world prematurely, sparking a war between Heaven and Hell that the demonic forces win, leaving the Earth in their claim. Brought before the Charred Council, War is accused of breaking sacred law and stripped of his powers. Facing his own death, he is given a chance to return to Earth and search for the truth behind the betrayal.

On the ruined surface, War is hunted by a vengeful group of Angels, must fight the armies of Hell, and forges uneasy alliances with the very demons he is hunting. The cast includes Vulgrim, a demon merchant who trades upgrades for souls, Ulthane, a master craftsman of the Makers, and the Watcher, an observer assigned by the Charred Council who accompanies War throughout his mission.

The game plays as an action-focused homage to The Legend of Zelda: a connected world of dungeons, puzzles and boss battles in which every new tool opens a path that was previously blocked. War's combat revolves around the Chaoseater sword, a growing arsenal that includes the Crossblade and the Abyssal Chain, and the powerful Chaos Form, supported by an upgrade system built on collected souls. Joe Madureira's bold, exaggerated comic-book art direction defines the look.

The walkthrough captures the full campaign in continuous cinematic form, presented without commentary on PS5 Pro. For viewers new to Darksiders: War's story is the first chapter of a four-horsemen saga that later turns to Death, Fury and Strife, and this opening game is a self-contained and compact way to start it.
