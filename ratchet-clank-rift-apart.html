Story

Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart is Insomniac Games' 2021 PS5 launch-window action-platformer, a structural sequel to the long-running Ratchet & Clank franchise that doubles as a self-contained introduction for new players. The campaign is staged three years after Into the Nexus (2013), with the franchise's signature Lombax mechanic Ratchet and his robot companion Clank now retired from active heroics and revered as celebrated former defenders of the multiverse. The opening sequence is the campaign's celebration ceremony — a public parade through Megalopolis honoring their accumulated heroics — where Clank reveals his rebuilt Dimensionator, the reality-traversing device the franchise's earlier installments established as the key to locating Ratchet's lost Lombax species.

The celebration is interrupted by Dr. Nefarious, the franchise's recurring mad-scientist antagonist, who steals the Dimensionator and attempts to use it to access a parallel dimension in which his counterpart is a successful conqueror. The Dimensionator is damaged during the confrontation, tearing the fabric of reality apart and scattering Ratchet, Clank, and Nefarious across multiple dimensional pockets. The campaign's central platforming hook is established in the opening hour: the player's traversal through the campaign's environments is structurally interleaved with rifts — small dimensional tears that the PS5's SSD streams through in real time, allowing the campaign to cut between environments mid-jump in a way the previous console generation could not technically support.

The campaign's middle act introduces the franchise's significant new protagonist: Rivet, a battle-scarred Lombax freedom fighter from the parallel dimension, who has been waging an underground resistance against this dimension's successful Emperor Nefarious. Rivet's missing arm — a battle scar she has carried for years — was inflicted by Kit, a Warbot Ratchet befriends in his own thread of the campaign. The campaign cuts between Ratchet's and Rivet's parallel investigations, with the two protagonists' first encounter — and their slow recognition that they are alternate-universe counterparts of each other — anchored by the franchise's most acclaimed character work to date. Kit's parallel arc with Clank, in which the Warbot confronts her history as Rivet's attacker and Clank quietly forgives her, gives the campaign's AI characters the emotional weight the franchise has previously reserved for its organic leads.

The campaign's third act converges on Emperor Nefarious's central capital — the parallel-dimension Megalopolis. The four protagonists coordinate a rebuilding of the Dimensionator, the recovery of the franchise's signature Lombax dimensional infrastructure, and the broader resistance coalition's coordinated assault on Emperor Nefarious's central palace. The campaign's most acclaimed mid-game environmental design — the Pocket Dimensions the rifts grant the player temporary access to, the Blizar Prime asteroid loop, the Sargasso wetlands, the Savali desert — provides the campaign's signature visual showcase.

The climactic sequence stages a coordinated battle in Megalopolis. Emperor Nefarious deploys a mechanized power suit; Ratchet destroys the suit; Rivet defeats the depowered Emperor in a sustained one-on-one duel; and the franchise's original Dr. Nefarious — increasingly irritated by his alternate self's smug dismissal of his own efforts — joins the protagonists in the final confrontation and sends Emperor Nefarious through a rift into permanent dimensional exile. The campaign closes with Rivet and Kit deciding to return home but agreeing to Ratchet's suggested side-trip first: the four of them activate the rebuilt Dimensionator together, with the closing image framing the quartet stepping through a portal presumably toward the long-promised Lombax dimension. The post-credits stinger frames the broader franchise's continuation, with Insomniac's stewardship of the series committed for the PS5 generation.
