Story

Silent Hill: Townfall is a full-length, self-contained psychological horror game released on September 24, 2026 for PlayStation 5, Steam and the Epic Games Store, co-published by Konami and Annapurna Interactive. It was developed by Screen Burn Interactive, the Scottish team formerly known as No Code, whose earlier work includes Stories Untold and the BAFTA-winning Observation. First announced in 2022, it is played entirely in first person.

The story begins in 1996 on the island of St. Amelia in Scotland. Simon Ordell has been called back to put things right. He arrives with little more than an IV bag and a medical wristband bearing his name, and heads toward a town lying quiet beneath a heavy fog — seemingly abandoned, but not at rest. Voices from a pocket television he picks up along the way are his only leads. As he ventures deeper, fragments of a past rise to the surface, and the mystery is built around his connection to the town and its inhabitants.

Townfall's design leans on vulnerability. Exploration and evasion drive much of the tension, combat is frenetic but limited by a restricted set of weapons and tools, and narrative-driven puzzles slowly uncover a truth that refuses to stay submerged. The signature device is the CRTV, a pocket television that tunes into unstable signals to reveal clues — a mechanic that fits the series' long tradition of using sound, static and broken media as horror language.

Townfall arrives in a period of renewed activity for the franchise. Konami has said it intends to release a new Silent Hill game roughly every year: Bloober Team's Silent Hill 2 remake launched in October 2024 and Silent Hill f followed in September 2025. Townfall takes a different approach from both. It is neither a remake nor a story set in 1960s Japan, but an original tale set in 1996 Scotland, built by a studio best known for atmospheric, narrative-led games.
