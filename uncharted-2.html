About the Game

Uncharted 2: Among Thieves is Naughty Dog's 2009 PS3 sequel — widely regarded as the title that defined the modern cinematic action-adventure genre. Nathan Drake, joined by Chloe Frazer and Elena Fisher, pursues the lost Cintamani Stone of Marco Polo's vanished fleet across Istanbul, Borneo, Nepal, and the Himalayan valley of Shambhala, hunted by the Serbian war criminal Zoran Lazarevic. The walkthrough captures the full campaign on PS5 Pro via backwards compatibility, with the franchise's signature globe-trotting set-piece direction at maximum visual fidelity, no commentary.

Walkthrough — Chapter Breakdown

A complete chapter-by-chapter overview of the campaign. Use this guide to track story progress, locate specific moments in the videos above, or plan a multi-session viewing.

  1. 1

    1. A Rock and a Hard Place

    The campaign opens in medias res with Drake bleeding out in a derailed train carriage hanging off a Tibetan cliff face. The opening sequence stages one of the franchise's most iconic introductions — the player learning the basic traversal mechanics while climbing through a vertically-suspended train interior. The chapter ends with Drake reaching solid ground in the Himalayan snowfall, the campaign's structural frame established as a sustained flashback.

  2. 2

    2. Borneo Expedition

    A flashback chapter staged in the Borneo jungle. Drake reunites with old partner Harry Flynn and meets Chloe Frazer on a museum-heist preparation. The chapter introduces the campaign's central artifact hunt: Marco Polo's lost fleet and the Cintamani Stone. The chapter dwells on the franchise's signature treasure-hunting fundamentals — environmental investigation, light combat, and the establishment of Drake's three-way working relationship with Flynn and Chloe.

  3. 3

    3. The Istanbul Museum Heist

    The campaign's first major action set-piece. Drake and Flynn infiltrate the Topkapi Palace museum in Istanbul to recover Marco Polo's oil lamp, the next clue in the Cintamani trail. The chapter weaves the franchise's signature climbing, stealth, and combat fundamentals into a sustained nighttime heist sequence. The chapter ends with Flynn's betrayal — Drake is left for the Turkish authorities, beginning a three-month prison sentence the campaign elides via cut to the Nepal arc.

  4. 4

    4. Nepal — Urban Warfare

    Drake, freed by Chloe and accompanied by reporter Elena Fisher and her cameraman Jeff, arrives in a war-torn Nepalese city occupied by Zoran Lazarevic's mercenary forces. The chapter introduces the campaign's most acclaimed combat environment — a partially destroyed city block where Drake fights through Lazarevic's troops while pursuing the next Cintamani clue. The chapter ends with the team escaping the city via a hotel rooftop sequence that has become canonical to the franchise's set-piece reputation.

  5. 5

    5. The Train

    The campaign's signature mid-game sequence. Drake infiltrates a Lazarevic supply train moving through Nepalese mountain country at high speed, fighting through carriages with the franchise's most acclaimed mobile combat design. The chapter dwells on the train's interior — cargo cars, troop carriers, and the central armored carriage where Lazarevic himself is staged. The chapter ends in the derailment sequence the campaign's opening flashed forward to, closing the structural loop.

  6. 6

    6. The Himalayan Village

    Drake, rescued from the train wreck by Tibetan villagers, recovers in a remote mountain village under the care of village elder Tenzin. The chapter is structured as a quiet exploration sequence with no combat, dedicated to character work and the village's relationship with the unnatural cold seeping down from the mountains above. The chapter ends with Drake and Tenzin departing for the higher slopes to investigate the source of the village's growing supernatural concerns.

  7. 7

    7. The Monastery

    A sustained exploration chapter set in an abandoned mountain monastery containing the next Cintamani clue. The chapter weaves combat against the Tibetan Yeti — the campaign's first sustained supernatural enemy — with the franchise's signature puzzle-solving and environmental investigation. The chapter ends with the path to Shambhala confirmed, the supernatural element of the Cintamani trail fully established.

  8. 8

    8. Shambhala — The Hidden Valley

    The campaign's third-act spectacle chapter. The hidden valley of Shambhala is revealed as a real architectural site — a tree-grown ancient city protected by the Tibetan Guardians. The chapter weaves combat against Lazarevic's mercenary forces with the franchise's signature treasure-hunting exploration, with the Guardians introduced as a third faction. The chapter ends with the Tree of Life — the source of the Cintamani Stone's power — confirmed, and Lazarevic's plan to drink its sap fully exposed.

  9. 9

    9. The Tree of Life — Final Confrontation

    The campaign's climax. Drake confronts Lazarevic, who has consumed the Tree of Life's sap and become functionally invulnerable to conventional weapons. The fight is staged inside the central Shambhala temple, weaving sustained combat against Lazarevic with the chapter's collapsing architectural design. The chapter ends with Lazarevic killed by the Tibetan Guardians' poison, the Tree of Life destroyed, and Shambhala sealed permanently behind the surviving party.

  10. 10

    10. Epilogue — Tibetan Village

    The campaign's epilogue is set in the surviving Tibetan village. Elena recovers from her injuries; Chloe departs alone; Drake and Elena's quiet conversation on a village rooftop closes the campaign on a note of unresolved romantic continuity. The credits roll over the franchise's signature musical cue, with the campaign's central character relationship positioned for the franchise's continuation.
